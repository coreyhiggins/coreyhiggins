### About

I am the founder and head of engineering at [Wynfall Studios](https://wynfall.dev). I built and train the Wynfall model family, and I run the engineering that turns it into software people use.

My work spans model training and inference, the desktop and terminal applications built on top of them, and the infrastructure that serves both. I care most about systems that stay honest under load: a model that reports what it actually did, a release that can be rolled back, and a privacy promise that holds on every plan rather than being a setting someone can turn off.

### Focus

- **Model training and evaluation**: building Sigel and measuring whether it is genuinely better, not just different.
- **Inference engineering**: serving production traffic on hardware I run myself.
- **Product engineering**: Wynfall Desktop, Wynfall Code, and the platform behind them.
