I founded [Wynfall Studios](https://wynfall.dev). I train the Wynfall model family on GPUs I run myself, and I build the products around it.

Most of the work stays closed, and that is the product. Nothing you give it trains a model, on any plan including Free, unless you send it to us as feedback. That rule is written into the Terms rather than tucked into a setting. The pipeline behind the models is not for sale.

<br>

## The model

**[Sigel](https://wynfall.dev)** is the Wynfall model. You choose how hard it thinks, from Fast answers on Free up to Max on Studio, and deeper thinking spends more of your usage rather than costing extra.

<br>

## Where it runs

| | |
|---|---|
| **[Chat](https://chat.wynfall.dev)** | In the browser. Chats stay in your browser, encrypted with a key that never leaves it. |
| **[Wynfall Desktop](https://wynfall.dev)** | Windows. Chats, files and projects in one window, with Sigel or a model running on your own machine. |
| **Wynfall Code** | Sigel in your terminal. It reads the project, runs the tests, asks before it acts. |
| **Wynfall Design** | Pictures from a description, made on your own PC. Nothing you make leaves the machine. |

<br>

## Limits you can count

Free is about six Sigel messages a day with no card and no time limit. Every paid plan states its usage in messages and coding hours, and no automatic overage charges are planned. When a plan runs out of room, Sigel waits for it to refill.

<br>

Production is a GH200. The lab is two 3090s, which is where the next thing gets figured out before it goes anywhere near a customer.

## Published tools

Small, zero-dependency utilities. Each exists because something bit me once, and they are the parts of this work that generalize.

| | |
|---|---|
| [**proofmark**](https://github.com/coreyhiggins/proofmark) | Backtest engine whose defaults make self-deception expensive. Suppresses impossible results, tests every bar for lookahead, counts your trials. |
| [**tanto**](https://github.com/coreyhiggins/tanto) | Measured Windows performance tuning with reversible changes and evidence from your own machine. |
| [**jvm-shutdown-watchdog**](https://github.com/coreyhiggins/jvm-shutdown-watchdog) | Forces a wedged JVM to exit and reports which threads wedged it. |
| [**linux-deploy-toolkit**](https://github.com/coreyhiggins/linux-deploy-toolkit) | Versioned releases, atomic symlink swap, health check, automatic rollback. |
| [**failsoft-webhook**](https://github.com/coreyhiggins/failsoft-webhook) | Posts alerts to a webhook without taking down the app sending them. |
| [**service-health-monitor**](https://github.com/coreyhiggins/service-health-monitor) | Endpoint and systemd monitoring on a timer, with state-change alerts. |
